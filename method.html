PEOPLE’S GRADE

Residents grade 1–10

Verified voters grade roads, water, health, education and bursaries, jobs and youth, corruption and availability. Each person has one current grade per leader per financial year. They can update it before June 30; the latest submission counts.

The overall score averages each voter’s answered categories, then averages eligible voters equally. Scores remain unpublished until the leader’s configured minimum is reached. Public pages show the count and date range.
